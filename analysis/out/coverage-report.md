## 9. Caveats and known data issues

- **Feb 29.** The M&E corpus HAS both slots for Feb 29 (`02-29-AM`, `02-29-PM`); the M'Cheyne plan does NOT (365 entries, no `0229`). Those 2 devotionals are excluded from every day-keyed number above — they cannot be joined. The app's current fallback is `dayOfYear(date) % 365` in `packages/core/src/bible/plan.ts`. What Feb 29 should show is a product decision, not settled here.
  - Feb 29 devotional references, for reference: `02-29-AM` → Jeremiah 31:3; `02-29-PM` → 1 Corinthians 2:12
- **Multi-reference devotionals.** `07-12-AM` carries 3 core text/reference pairs and `08-30-PM` carries 2. All references participate in the join; `coreText`/`reference` hold the first, `coreTexts`/`references` hold all.
- **Reference fixups applied:** 1. `Luke 4.18 -> Luke 4:18` — one reference uses a period where every other uses a colon; the repo parser rejects it as-is.
- **Unparsed devotional references:** 0.
- **Unparsed plan readings:** 0.
- **Cross-chapter tail chapters:** 48 chapter-slots come from verse-scoped refs (e.g. `12:1-13:1` drags in chapter 13 for one verse; `119:1-24` covers part of Psalm 119). They are counted as full chapters, matching `expandReadings()` in the app. This slightly inflates the denominator.
- **Single-chapter books.** In `Jude 24`, `2 John 2`, `Philemon 2`, `3 John 3` the number is a verse, not a chapter. The repo parser's `SINGLE_CHAPTER_BOOKS` set resolves these to chapter 1 correctly.
- **Source transcription defect:** `04-14-PM` contains a doubled underscore (`__well upon divine`) — the only entry with unbalanced italic markers. Preserved as-is rather than silently "fixed".
- **`--` is not a reference marker.** 739 lines in the source begin with `--` but only 735 are references; 4 are body prose using `--` as a leading em-dash. The parser discriminates on right-padding (indent ≥ 20), not on the `--` prefix.
