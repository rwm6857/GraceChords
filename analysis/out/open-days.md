# Open Days

37 of the 365 M'Cheyne days have **no** Spurgeon devotional keyed to
anything they read. Nothing was invented to cover them — they are genuinely
empty, and this list is what an empty devotional slot has to account for.

For each date below, every devotional in the corpus was checked against every
chapter the plan reads that day. The `Eligible candidates` column is the count
that survived; it is 0 on every row, which is why the day is open.

Two distinct causes, separated in the last column:

- **no devotional on these chapters** — the corpus never touches any chapter read
  that day. Nothing about the matching rules would change this.
- **rejected by the verse-level rule** — a devotional does reference one of these
  chapters, but the plan reads only part of that chapter and the devotional's
  verse falls outside the part read. Relaxing the rule to chapter-level would
  fill the day, at the cost of pairing text the reader never reached.
